# Procedure: Temperature Sensor (TS1–TS4) Anomaly

**Failure mode:** Elevated or erratic temperature readings from TS1–TS4.

**Typical signature:** Gradual upward drift in temperature importance over
several prediction windows, often co-occurring with elevated EPS1 (motor power).

**First checks:**
1. Check cooling system airflow and fan operation.
2. Inspect for oil degradation — old hydraulic fluid loses thermal conductivity.
3. Cross-reference with EPS1 (motor power) — sustained high motor load combined
   with rising temperature suggests the motor is working harder than normal,
   often due to internal leakage (see pump procedure below).

**Escalation:** Persistent thermal anomaly with no cooling-system fault found
should trigger a full pump inspection.
