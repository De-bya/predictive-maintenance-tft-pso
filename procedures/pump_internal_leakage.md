# Procedure: Pump Internal Leakage (maps to dataset 'pump' label)

**Failure mode:** Internal leakage severity levels 0 (none), 1 (weak), 2 (severe).

**Typical signature:** Combined pressure drop (PS sensors) and flow reduction
(FS1/FS2) without a corresponding drop in motor power — the pump is working
but not delivering proportional output.

**First checks:**
1. Measure actual flow output (FS1/FS2) against expected flow for the current
   motor RPM/power (EPS1).
2. Inspect pump seals and check for oil in the drain line, indicating internal
   bypass.
3. Check accumulator pre-charge pressure — a failing accumulator can mimic
   pump leakage symptoms.

**Escalation:** Severity 2 (severe leakage) requires immediate pump service;
severity 1 can typically be scheduled for the next maintenance window.
