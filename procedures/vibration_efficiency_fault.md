# Procedure: Vibration & Efficiency Sensor Anomaly (VS1, CE, CP, SE)

**Failure mode:** Elevated vibration (VS1) or degraded cooling
efficiency/contamination readings (CE, CP, SE).

**Typical signature:** VS1 importance spikes often co-occur with pump or
motor bearing wear; CE/CP/SE anomalies typically point to filter or coolant
degradation rather than a mechanical fault.

**First checks:**
1. For VS1: inspect motor and pump mounting bolts and bearing condition.
2. For CE/CP/SE: check filter saturation and coolant/oil contamination
   levels; replace filters if due.
3. Cross-check with temperature procedure — contamination and cooling issues
   often present together with rising TS readings.
