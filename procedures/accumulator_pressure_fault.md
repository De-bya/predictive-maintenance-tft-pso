# Procedure: Accumulator Pressure Anomaly

**Failure mode:** Accumulator pressure readings outside the 90/100/115/130 bar
nominal bands.

**Typical signature:** Slow pressure decay between pump cycles, or the system
pressure sagging more than expected under load.

**First checks:**
1. Check nitrogen pre-charge pressure with the system fully depressurized.
2. Inspect the bladder/piston for wear if pre-charge is correct but pressure
   still decays under load.
3. Cross-check with pump leakage procedure — accumulator and pump faults can
   present with similar downstream pressure symptoms.
